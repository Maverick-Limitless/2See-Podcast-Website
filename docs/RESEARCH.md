## Verified facts

- **Public name**: 2See Podcast. Channel handle `@2See.Podcast`, channel ID
  `UCIV4JGPrV3bHNBGYPLnfqTA`.
- **Persian name**: «توسی» (Tosi), used throughout the episode descriptions.
- **Founded**: the channel's feed reports a channel publication date of
  15 November 2025.
- **Scale**: 27.6k subscribers and 140 videos at the time of research.
- **Stated purpose**, quoted from the channel description:
  > "Welcome to 2See, where two classmates explore the world through curiosity,
  > humor, and deep thinking. … the podcast brings together two academic paths
  > and one shared love of learning. 2See Podcast is at the meeting point of
  > technology, business, and policy. Our goal is to make complex ideas
  > understandable and connect classroom thinking to real life."
- **Hosts**, quoted from the channel description:
  - Saleh Dadkhahipour — MBA, Stanford GSB; MPA, Harvard Kennedy School
  - Mehrzad Sajjadi — MBA & MS CS Stanford; Biotechnology master's, Harvard
- **Persian positioning**, quoted verbatim:
  > "به توسی خوش آمدید؛ جایی که دو دانشجوی ایرانی از استنفورد و هاروارد به
  > فناوری، تجارت و سیاست‌گذاری می پردازند. …"
- **Formats observed in the feed**:
  - Long Persian-language conversations with named guests (e.g. episode 42 with
    میلاد منشی‌پور).
  - A weekly English briefing, "2See Brief", labelled by calendar week
    (e.g. CW41).
  - Persian- and English-language Shorts.
- **Topics** in recent episodes: economic growth comparisons between Iran,
  Türkiye and South Korea; US federal debt and the Strait of Hormuz; NASA's
  Roman Space Telescope; NVIDIA's position in China; export controls; the
  Nobel Prizes; Google's nuclear energy deal; AI price competition.
- **Publication cadence**: the feed shows a new long episode roughly weekly,
  with several Shorts between them.

## Design implications

- The name itself encodes a pair — two classmates, two academic paths, two
  registers of output (a Persian long-form conversation and an English weekly
  brief). **Assumption:** this pairing is the site's structural idea: content
  is presented twice, once at length and once in brief.
- Artwork uses a near-black navy ground, warm amber highlight words, and a
  secondary violet data line on chart motifs. **Assumption:** the amber is the
  single accent; the violet is left to the real thumbnails rather than promoted
  into the design system, which keeps the palette to one restrained accent.
- Episode numbering is real metadata (اپیزود ۴۳ = episode 43), so numbers are
  shown as structure, not decoration.

## Asset use and limits

- Episode titles, descriptions, publication dates and thumbnails are publicly
  published by the channel and reproduced here as metadata, unmodified, with a
  link back to the original video.
- No photograph, logo or artwork beyond the publicly served video thumbnails is
  used, and no permission to use protected photography is claimed.
- The channel feed provides no duration data and only the most recent uploads;
  duration and full archive paging require the YouTube Data API (see
  docs/DEPLOYMENT.md).
- Nothing in this project implies endorsement by, or affiliation with, the
  programme or its hosts.

## Content integrity

No episodes, guests, quotes, view counts, ratings or biographies were invented.
Every episode on the demo site comes from the channel's own feed. Where the
feed provided no description, the entry says so rather than filling a gap.
